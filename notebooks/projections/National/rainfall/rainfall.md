# Rainfall

Projected rainfall indicators at the selected site from the CSIRO CMIP6 multi-model ensemble (grid cell nearest to the site on a common 1° grid). Rainfall changes are uncertain in sign across models for many Pacific islands, so the spread across models is as important as the median.
