# Tropical cyclones

Global climate models do not resolve tropical cyclones well enough to project local statistics directly. This section illustrates a plausible change in exposure by shifting historical IBTrACS tracks poleward by the projected migration of the latitude of maximum cyclone intensity, and comparing the storms that pass close to the site.
