# Tropical cyclones

Global climate models do not resolve tropical cyclones well enough to project local statistics directly. This section illustrates a plausible change in exposure by shifting historical IBTrACS tracks poleward by the projected migration of cyclone genesis latitude over the western North Pacific (+1.13°, CMIP6-HighResMIP, 1980–2010 to 2020–2050 under SSP5-8.5; [Li & Zhou, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41612-024-00704-3)), and comparing the storms that pass close to the site.
