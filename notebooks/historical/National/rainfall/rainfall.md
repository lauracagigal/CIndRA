# Rainfall

Rainfall availability and extremes influence water security, agriculture, ecosystems and flood risk across Pacific islands. This section uses quality-controlled daily station observations and applies completeness filters before calculating annual indicators.

Run the [national atmosphere setup](../00_site_setup.ipynb) first to select the station and create the cached precipitation record.

## How to use the results

Compare annual values with the climatological reference, inspect missing-data diagnostics, and consider ENSO and other modes of Pacific climate variability when interpreting unusually wet or dry years. Trends should only be reported when temporal coverage and station consistency are adequate.
