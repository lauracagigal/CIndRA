# Tropical cyclones

Tropical cyclones produce damaging winds, waves, storm surge and intense rainfall. Their impacts can extend far beyond the track centre, so the national analysis selects systems entering a configurable radius around the study site and describes their historical occurrence and intensity using NOAA IBTrACS.
