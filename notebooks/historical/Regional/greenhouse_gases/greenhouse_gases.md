# Greenhouse gases

The concentration of carbon dioxide (CO₂) in the atmosphere is the benchmark indicator of
human-driven climate change: rising greenhouse gases drive the changes in temperature, rainfall,
sea level and ocean chemistry described by the other indicators of this book.
