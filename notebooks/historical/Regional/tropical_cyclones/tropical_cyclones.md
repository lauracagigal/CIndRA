# Regional tropical cyclones

Tropical-cyclone climatology differs substantially across the Pacific. This workflow uses the all-basin NOAA IBTrACS archive to compare the Western North, Central North, Western South and Central South Pacific with a consistent classification and analysis period.

## Figures and indicators included

- **Tracks by subregion:** geographical distribution of observed cyclone trajectories, coloured consistently by Pacific region.
- **Monthly genesis seasonality:** mean cyclones per month, separated into named-only, cyclone/typhoon/hurricane and major-system intensity classes.
- **Spatial track density:** heat maps showing where cyclone observations occur most frequently without obscuring patterns through overlapping tracks.
- **Comparison between periods:** boxplots for homogeneous decadal windows (1985–1995, 1995–2005, 2005–2015 and 2015–2025).
- **Annual activity and intensity:** named-system counts, intensity composition, five-year means and linear trends.
- **Accumulated Cyclone Energy:** a complementary measure combining storm intensity and duration.

The plots use a distinct regional colour family throughout the section. Equivalent wind thresholds are shown in knots so intensity categories remain explicit when terminology changes between typhoon, hurricane and cyclone basins.
