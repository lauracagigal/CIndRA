# Regional air temperature

Air temperature is one of the clearest indicators of a changing climate. The regional workflow places individual station records in a Pacific-wide context, allowing users to compare warming, heat and cool extremes among islands with different climates and observational histories.

Run the [regional setup](../00_regional_setup.ipynb) first to construct the multi-station dataset.

## What you will find

The notebook harmonizes station records, applies data-availability criteria and summarizes changes in annual temperature metrics. Maps reveal spatial differences, while time series retain the history at each monitoring location. ERA5 fields provide regional context but do not replace the station observations used for the indicators.

```{note}
Differences in station period, missing observations, relocation or instrumentation can affect comparisons. Review the quality-control and completeness outputs before interpreting regional contrasts.
```
