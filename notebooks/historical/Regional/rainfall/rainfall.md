# Regional rainfall

Pacific rainfall varies strongly over space and time because of the Intertropical and South Pacific Convergence Zones, monsoons, tropical cyclones and ENSO. The regional analysis compares station-based rainfall indicators while retaining the local records needed to understand these contrasts.

Run the [regional setup](../00_regional_setup.ipynb) first to prepare the station dictionary and analysis metadata.

## What you will find

The workflow applies common completeness criteria to multiple station records and visualizes how rainfall totals and extremes differ across the Pacific. The maps help identify coherent regional patterns, whereas the station outputs preserve local variability that can be smoothed in gridded reanalysis products.

Use the results to compare broad spatial behaviour, but check record length, missingness and station metadata before drawing conclusions about differences between countries or long-term trends.
