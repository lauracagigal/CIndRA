Key pattern differences to notice:
  - Rainfall/air-temperature: figures and tables/JSON are split into
    outputs/figures/<site_tag>/ and outputs/tables/<site_tag>/, and every
    filename is suffixed with _<site_tag> via build_output_filename().
  - Sea level: everything (PNG, HTML, CSV, JSON) lands directly in
    outputs/<site_tag>/, and filenames are NOT suffixed with _<site_tag>
    (no build_output_filename() call) -- the per-site folder is what
    prevents collisions instead.
  - Regional: no per-site folder at all -- one shared outputs/figures/regional_<region_key>/
    folder, filenames suffixed with _<region_key> instead of a site tag.
